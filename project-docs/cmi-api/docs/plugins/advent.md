# Advent

Advent is an independent Christmas activity for 2026. Its progress, reward claims, NPC bindings and preview records belong to Advent; Halloween, GhostHunt, Hide & Sheep and other activity records are not prerequisites or migration sources.

The [November 4 memory investigation](../compatibility-tests/advent-memory-26.3-2026-11-04.md) records the live incident evidence, fresh Santa/Citizens local comparison, and bounded-storage/preview-lifecycle fixes. The original live crash is not yet attributed to a retaining component; a short stable local run is not production-load acceptance. Progress writes now fail with a busy response when 256 operations are outstanding, while open/click statistics share durable batches. Rejected reservations cannot authorize delivery, and accepted writes drain during shutdown.

This document describes the implemented command and configuration surface and retains the outstanding acceptance requirements. Verification is tracked in [the 2026 plan](../plans/advent-2026.md) and [the October 29 compatibility record](../compatibility-tests/advent-26.3-2026-10-29.md). The October 28 build 822 and early October 29 build 823 results are historical baselines. Build **825** includes postcard notifications, passport souvenirs, staff book archives and actor relocation. It passed the canonical clean full-suite build and local Paper 26.3 build 227 checks, including native signed-book serialization and actor persistence across restart. Implementation does not by itself mean that every feature has passed gameplay acceptance.

## Player FAQ

### How do I unlock a day's present?

During December 1–24, find that day's elf in Santa's world, then return to Santa and select today's present. `/advent elf` gives a landmark clue. You must complete both steps on the same Europe/Amsterdam calendar day; opening `/advent` alone cannot unlock it. A missed day cannot be caught up later.

### Must I collect a reward immediately?

No. An unlocked present stays available in `/advent claim` from any world while you are alive in Survival mode. Collect all earned rewards before **January 1, 2027 at 00:00 Europe/Amsterdam**. There is no January grace period. Your menu distinguishes an unlocked day from a reward already collected.

### What else can I browse?

`/advent stories`, `/advent passport`, `/advent kindness` and `/advent community` show the unlocked stories, your seasonal record, approved postcards and shared progress. `/advent mitten` shows the optional quest when staff have enabled it. Final activity and reward availability follow the server's setup.

### What if progress is busy or a reward needs attention?

Wait for the current save or claim to finish, then reopen the menu. A busy response does not authorize another reward. If a claim remains pending or needs review, keep its reference for staff; repeated clicks cannot safely replay an uncertain delivery.

## Daily visit and unlock

There are 24 elves at 24 distinct locations in the Santa world. All 24 remain visible throughout December; only the current day's elf grants progress when right-clicked. Each elf is assigned to exactly one date from December 1 through December 24 and the matching Advent day. A player must find and right-click that day's elf on that exact date to earn the visit prerequisite. Elf 1 counts only on December 1 for Advent 1, elf 2 only on December 2 for Advent 2, and so on; clicking either of those elves on December 3 does not count. They then right-click Santa in his workshop to open the interactive Advent calendar and right-click that date's present to unlock it. The unlocked day also opens its story chapter. Progress is individual, identified by the player's authoritative server UUID, including Java and Floodgate players.

An unlock is available only on its exact calendar date. Finding a future elf does not unlock a future day, and a missed date cannot be caught up later. Date, permissions, world, actor binding, visit prerequisite and existing progress must be rechecked when the player acts, including after midnight or after a configuration reload. The owner-approved timezone is `Europe/Amsterdam`: each day runs from Dutch midnight until the next Dutch midnight for every player, regardless of their location. The configured season and timezone, rather than the host machine's implicit timezone or the player's local date, determine the date.

Only an authenticated Santa interaction in the configured Santa world grants an interactive unlock session. A menu title, renamed item, matching NPC name or client-side inventory arrangement does not establish that authority. A regular `/advent` menu opened elsewhere remains useful for browsing and planning but cannot grant an unlock.
