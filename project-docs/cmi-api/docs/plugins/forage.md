# Forage

Forage turns survival gathering into a longer adventure: build your own camp, use specialized tools, discover plants, complete quests and help your community finish seasonal projects. Player XP, Forage Points and reward history follow you between supported worlds; each camp has its own Local Introduction before you use its full services.

Start with `/forage next`, or open `/forage` for the overview. The complete player guide explains each step and the current in-game menus show availability, prices and limits.

## What you can work toward

There are eight Forage tool types, three camp slots, personal and tool progression, skill branches, daily/weekly/monthly quests, a one-time participation reward with four map-art pieces, twelve Herbarium collection rewards and quarterly Community Efforts. Camp services include Repair & Merge, tool upgrades and approved enchantments, resource Turn-Ins and Forage Dust crafting. Brush activities add archaeology, timed sifting, relic cleaning, fossil surveys and armadillo grooming.

Explore at your own pace. Use `/forage inspect` on a nearby block or `/forage source <name>` before guessing which tool to use. For a quest, open **What Counts?** and **Filter by Tool** to see the exact routes. Browse away from camp; return to your own active composter workstation to claim rewards or spend resources.
