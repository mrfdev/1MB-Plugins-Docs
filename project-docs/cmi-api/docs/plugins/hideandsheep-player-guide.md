# Hide and Sheep: meet Woolbur

Woolbur, Charlie the Vote Llama's mischievous nephew, has arrived with an unusual talent: being the wrong size at exactly the wrong moment. Hide and Sheep is a timed community search exclusive to 1MB. Find Woolbur among his normal-sized decoys, touch him, and earn points for the reward shop.

## How Players Use It

Run `/sheep` when the event announcement appears. The menu tells you whether an event is live, where it is, how far to search, and how much time remains. Choose **Join** to enter that round and reach the arrival point, including while everyone is gathering. You must join each new round before touching Woolbur or clearing decoys can earn points; simply walking into the area does not enter you.

Watch for the aqua-blue start and finish announcements, separated from other chat by a blank line above and below.

Between rounds, the card says **No event running** and **Woolbur is resting**. During preparation it says **Starting soon**, and while a round finishes it says **Round ending**. After the event opens, travel is available during both gathering and searching. The menu updates automatically; clicking a waiting card simply checks the latest status.

Available features include:

- A light-blue menu with live event details and joining.
- The same search rules for every participant, including staff and higher ranks.
- Eight costumes, including upside-down and colour-changing sheep from half-size to double-size.
- A bigger or smaller real sheep among normal-sized decoys.
- One to five bonus points for every distinct decoy you clear, depending on the round.
- Sheep celebrations with sounds and particles when you find or clear one.
- A gathering countdown, normally two minutes, followed by the search countdown and public results.
- Time reminders, occasional nearby baas, and a short glow hint near the end.
- Personal participation, finds, lifetime points, and spendable points.
- A reward shop with community feature points and survival supply kits.
- A twenty-page story book about Woolbur and Uncle Charlie, with shorter pages that fit the book.

The bottom-left player head shows your statistics and returns to the event menu. The middle book explains the game. The shining nether star, directly left of the bottom-right barrier, returns to `/menu`; the barrier closes the window.

## Your first search

Each round has one real Woolbur; the first three different players to find that same sheep earn rewards.

1. Run `/sheep` and check that the event is live.
2. Choose **Join**, or run `/sheep join`, and wait for your entry to complete.
3. During gathering, clear inventory, teleport, and let the area load. Woolbur normally hides **120 seconds** after the event opens; the boss bar shows the time remaining.
4. When the search announcement appears, search within the displayed radius of the arrival point. The normal-regular radius is 128 blocks; other rounds can be smaller or larger.
5. Look for the sheep that is noticeably bigger or smaller. The costume alone is not the answer: decoys can wear it too.
6. Walk into Woolbur, right-click him, or tap him from close range. Each player can earn one Woolbur finder reward in that event.

After you have joined that round, protected regions still allow the game to record your discovery. You do not need to damage the sheep or change any blocks. Normal sheep elsewhere in the world are not part of the game.

Woolbur keeps his name hidden until you find him. Once your points are awarded, you can see his name above him while nearby with a clear view. This reveal is yours alone; players still searching can use his size and the round's occasional clues to spot him. Decoys do not reveal a name.

The default first prize is **100 points**, with **50 points** for second and **25 points** for third. Staff can adjust the first prize. The second reward is half, and the third is a quarter, of that first prize. The event ends after three different players find Woolbur or the timer expires. The normal search time limit is **six minutes**, starting after gathering ends. The gathering countdown does not use up your search time. Sheep appear and points become available only when searching begins. If nobody finds him, Woolbur wins.

## Fair play and leaving

Joining temporarily switches off perk flight and resets custom walking speed. While you participate, teleport shortcuts, compass navigation, TPA, flight, speed, game-mode and vanish perks are blocked. Staff and higher ranks follow these rules too. **Ender pearls and elytra remain allowed.**

Friends cannot send or receive TPA requests with you while you play. Command and plugin teleports also cannot bring someone outside the event within **two blocks** of a participant, even if their teleport was already counting down. Other command/plugin arrivals inside that small distance are blocked too; the rest of the area stays open. Joining through `/sheep` still works, and pearls and elytra remain allowed. Staff needing a host teleport must leave the round first; a participating destination player must leave too.

Use `/sheep leave` when you need your normal travel and perks again. You keep your earned points and history, but cannot rejoin that same round. Death or changing worlds also ends your participation. Disconnecting does not remove these rules; reconnecting during the same round continues your entry if it is still valid.

Restrictions end when the round finishes. Your normal permissions remain yours throughout; flight and custom walking speed are not automatically switched back on afterward. You can enable those perks again normally after leaving. If entry or cleanup cannot complete, the game explains the problem instead of allowing unrestricted scoring.

## Clearing decoys

After joining, touch a normal-size event decoy to clear it for everyone and earn bonus points. The number of decoys at the **start of the round** sets the reward for each:

- **1–2 decoys:** 5 points each.
- **3–4 decoys:** 4 points each.
- **5–8 decoys:** 3 points each.
- **9–12 decoys:** 2 points each.
- **13–16 decoys:** 1 point each.

That rate stays the same as decoys disappear. Every different decoy you clear pays again; there is no per-player round limit. Each decoy can be cleared and rewarded only once, so only its first successful clearer earns those points. Chat announces who cleared it, the points awarded, and how many remain.

You can earn decoy points as well as a Woolbur reward. With the twelve normal-regular decoys, clearing all twelve earns 24 points; finding Woolbur first as well brings your total to **124 points**. You keep your decoy points even if you never find Woolbur. Decoys do not count as finding him or claim one of the three finder rewards.

A little sheep rises with sounds and particles to celebrate each successful find or clear. The real Woolbur stays in place for other players to find the same sheep. Cleared decoys disappear for everyone, and touching a celebration sheep gives no reward.

## Clues during the search

Chat reminds you when two minutes, one minute, thirty seconds, and ten seconds remain. Short rounds skip any reminder that would coincide with or precede their start. The boss bar always shows the current countdown.

After the first ten search seconds, you may hear a baa when you are within **32 blocks** of Woolbur. It is a private clue for players who have not found him, limited to once every **45 seconds** and at most **three times per round**.

If finder places remain, Woolbur glows briefly near the end: the final **5% of search time**, with a minimum of five seconds and a maximum of thirty. That means six seconds for Speedrun, twelve for High Difficulty, and eighteen for Normal Regular. Keep looking for the unusual size until then!

Staff can choose different outings. **Speedrun** has a shorter wait, a smaller arena, and a giant Woolbur worth 200 points for first place. **High Difficulty** sends you after a half-size sheep in a wide arena for 500 first-place points. **Normal Regular** is the six-minute outing with a 100-point first prize. Check the event menu for the current rules; your points stay with you between rounds.

## Your points and rewards

`/sheep stats` shows how many events you have participated in, how often you found Woolbur, your lifetime earned points, and the balance you can spend. Successfully joining counts as participation once in that round. Opening the menu or touching a sheep without joining does not count. Only finding Woolbur increases your found count; earned points and spendable balance also include decoy bonuses. Spending points does not reduce your lifetime total or erase past finds.

Open `/sheep rewards` to browse **Point exchanges** on their own row and **Item kits** below. Each offer shows its type, cost in Hide and Sheep points, and what you receive. Choose an offer to review the same details before confirming. Kit previews reflect the server's configured contents; unavailable kits are clearly labelled.

Point exchanges include DiscordChat, AFKShrine, Forage, AutoSell Broker Points, and Appreciation Points. The default offers exchange **100 sheep points for 1 Broker Point** or **200 sheep points for 1 Appreciation Point**; check the menu for the server's current prices. These add spendable points without advancing AutoSell sales or levels, sending appreciations, or completing streaks.

The six item kits start with **Woolbur's Pocket Snacks**. The supplied **6-point** kit contains two **Woolbur's Pocket Cookies** and one **Woolbur's Crunchy Apple**; staff can customize the items, so check its preview. Clearing three decoys in a Normal Regular round earns enough, even if you never find Woolbur. You can buy it again whenever you have enough points and meet the usual claim requirements.

If staff changes a kit while you are confirming it, reopen the reward to review its new contents before spending your points.

Selected kit items have light-blue Woolbur names and short lore, such as **Woolbur Watcher** (spyglass) and **Woolbur's Fringe Fixer** (shears). These are cosmetic touches on ordinary items; the compass does not track Woolbur.

Feature-point exchanges require their receiving feature to be available. Supply kits require CMI and the server's Forage inventory-saving service; you do not need to be at a Forage Camp. If the service is unavailable, the shop explains why and does not take your points. Claims are permitted only in the configured worlds for these game modes: End, Nether, Wild, General, Cave, Acid, OneBlock, SkyBlock, SkyGrid, and ChunkBlock. Event travel and searching are not limited to this list.

Make room in your inventory and turn off AutoSell before claiming item rewards. A blocked claim shows what needs to change. If a previous delivery is waiting for staff review, ask staff for help with its reference instead of trying to buy it again.

## Player FAQ

### Why does the menu say there is no live event?

Staff prepare and start each event. **No event running** means Woolbur is resting between outings; it is not an invitation to teleport. Wait for the next announcement, then choose **Join the gathering**, or **Join the live event** if searching has already started.

### The event is live, but where are the sheep?

The first announcement opens a gathering period, normally **120 seconds**, so everyone has time to clear inventory, teleport, and load in. Woolbur and his decoys appear when that countdown ends. Watch the boss bar and the search announcement. Staff may shorten or skip gathering for another round.

### Will every round happen in the same place?

Staff can save different locations and round settings for Woolbur's visits. Check `/sheep` after each announcement for the current event and its travel button. Each round chooses fresh random safe hiding spots within its prepared radius. A previous spot can be picked again by chance. Your earned points and reward balance stay with you between locations.

### Is the rainbow sheep always the right one?

No. Upside-down sheep and changing colours are costumes. The real Woolbur is the sheep with a noticeably different size. All event decoys remain normal-sized adults.

### Does clicking in a protected region count?

Yes. A close interaction with the actual event sheep counts even if a region plugin cancels ordinary interaction. You can also walk into him. You must have joined this round, be alive, and be playing in Survival or Adventure mode.

### Why did touching a decoy or Woolbur do nothing?

You must join the current round through `/sheep` or `/sheep join` first, and searching must have begun. Being nearby or having played an earlier round is not enough. A cleared decoy cannot be rewarded again, and each player can find Woolbur only once per round.

### Why can I not use my compass, /jump or /tpa?

These shortcuts would give some ranks an advantage. They are temporarily blocked for everyone who joins, along with other teleport and flight perks. Use an ender pearl or elytra, or leave with `/sheep leave` to restore normal travel. Leaving means waiting for the next round to play again.

### Can I get all three places by touching Woolbur repeatedly?

No. One player can earn one place in each event. Reconnecting, changing worlds, or switching hands does not create another place.

### What if only one or two players find him?

Those players keep their points. The search remains open until the timer expires or the third finder completes it.

### Why do my earned points and available points differ?

Earned points are your lifetime total. Available points are what remains after reward purchases and reservations. Use `/sheep stats` to see both.

### Why can I play here but not claim a reward here?

The event can be held in any prepared world. Reward claims use a separate world allowlist to protect the server's game-mode economies. Move to one of the approved claim worlds and reopen `/sheep rewards`.

### What happens when the server restarts?

An interrupted gathering countdown or search does not restart automatically. Your saved participation, finds, and awarded points remain. The plugin cleans up the interrupted round's temporary restrictions, and staff can prepare a fresh outing.
