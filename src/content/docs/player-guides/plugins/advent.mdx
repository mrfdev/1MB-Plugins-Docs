This page introduces Advent from a public server-docs point of view. It focuses on what the feature does in-game, how to get started when you have access, and which commands are useful.

## What It Does

When staff open Advent, visit Santa during December 1–24, find the current elf and return to unlock that day’s present. Earned rewards can be collected later before the season closes.

## How Players Use It

Open `/advent` to browse the calendar and activities. Use `/advent elf` for the daily landmark clue, then interact with Santa after finding that day’s elf. The command menu is for browsing and cannot unlock a present on its own.

Available features include:

- One exact-date elf discovery and Santa present unlock for each of December’s first 24 days.
- Separate earned-reward claims, with a preview and confirmation for any choice.
- Stories and a personal Christmas passport with cumulative stamps.
- Approved Kindness postcards, community progress and an optional Missing Mitten quest.
- Clear available, unlocked, missed and delivered states in the calendar and reward menus.

## Quick Start

1. Use `/advent` to check the season, today’s progress and available activities.
2. During an open December 1–24 day, use `/advent elf`, find its elf and return to Santa.
3. Unlock that same day’s present through Santa, then use `/advent claim` for earned rewards.
4. Collect everything before January 1, 2027 at 00:00 Europe/Amsterdam.

## Player FAQ

### How do I unlock a day's present?

During December 1–24, find that day's elf in Santa's world, then return to Santa and select today's present. `/advent elf` gives a landmark clue. You must complete both steps on the same Europe/Amsterdam calendar day; opening `/advent` alone cannot unlock it. A missed day cannot be caught up later.

### Must I collect a reward immediately?

No. An unlocked present stays available in `/advent claim` from any world while you are alive in Survival mode. Collect all earned rewards before **January 1, 2027 at 00:00 Europe/Amsterdam**. There is no January grace period. Your menu distinguishes an unlocked day from a reward already collected.

### What else can I browse?

`/advent stories`, `/advent passport`, `/advent kindness` and `/advent community` show the unlocked stories, your seasonal record, approved postcards and shared progress. `/advent mitten` shows the optional quest when staff have enabled it. Final activity and reward availability follow the server's setup.

### What if progress is busy or a reward needs attention?

Wait for the current save or claim to finish, then reopen the menu. A busy response does not authorize another reward. If a claim remains pending or needs review, keep its reference for staff; repeated clicks cannot safely replay an uncertain delivery.

## Good To Know

- Dates use Europe/Amsterdam. Find the elf and unlock the present on the same day; missed days cannot be caught up later.
- Earned claims remain available from any world to living Survival players until the exclusive January 1 cutoff.
- Public progression and optional activities depend on staff activation and the server’s finalized reward setup.
