This page introduces Halloween Ghost Hunt from a public server-docs point of view. It focuses on what the feature does in-game, how to get started when you have access, and which commands are useful.

## What It Does

Halloween Ghost Hunt is part of Event Hunts. The 2026 hunt runs October 29 through November 1, with four cumulative waves of six Ghosts: 24 in total. Each completed wave earns its own kit, and all 24 unlock the completion bonus.

## How Players Use It

Open `/ghosthunt` for progress, earned rewards and the Ghost Points shop, or `/hunt` for all seasonal activities. During hunting dates, right-click an unlocked Ghost head with your main hand. Each Ghost counts once for you and remains available to others.

Available features include:

- Four six-Ghost waves with catch-up access until hunting ends.
- Four independent wave kits and a separate all-24 completion bonus.
- Two one-time item bundles at 20 Ghost Points each.
- One permanent choice of Bat, Fox or Cat MobHat for 24 points per edition.
- A confirmation page showing the current price and actual bundle contents.
- Private full, reduced or off effects and coordinate-free hints during the hunt.
- Saved progress and reward claims after hunting ends, through the configured deadline.

## Quick Start

1. Open `/ghosthunt` to check your saved progress and the current event phase.
2. During the hunt, visit Halloween and right-click unlocked Ghosts; use `/ghosthunt hint` if needed.
3. Use `/ghosthunt rewards` for earned wave kits and the completion bonus.
4. Use `/ghosthunt shop` to preview bundles or your one MobHat choice before confirming.

## Player FAQ

### How do I catch a Ghost or get a hint?

During the event, right-click an unlocked Ghost with your main hand. It counts once for you and remains available for others. `/ghosthunt hint` gives direction and distance guidance toward an eligible uncaught Ghost, with a cooldown.

### Can I catch up on a missed day and still earn every daily kit?

Yes. Complete the six heads assigned to each unlocked wave before the hunt ends. Completing both day 1 and day 2 on day 2 earns both kits. Completing all four waves on the final hunt day earns all four kits and the all-24 bonus.

### What is required for the perfect-weekend reward?

The 2026 bonus requires all 24 Ghosts, regardless of the dates on which they were found. Catching up all 24 on the final date qualifies for the bonus. Each wave’s six heads also earns that wave’s kit, including when completed later.

### How do I collect earned kits?

Open `/ghosthunt rewards` and claim in a configured reward world before the edition’s claim deadline. Read any AutoSell or delivery message; a reward needing staff attention is not another collection goal. The bundled 2026 weekend profile awards four wave kits and one completion bonus. Earned kits remain claimable through November 10 inclusive.

### Where can I spend Ghost Points?

Open `/ghosthunt shop` for two item bundles costing 20 points each and one choice of Bat, Fox, or Cat MobHat costing 24 points. Each bundle can be purchased once per edition, and only one of the three hats can be selected. The shop closes with earned-kit claims after November 10. See the [November 2026 shop details](#november-2026-ghost-point-shop) for contents and delivery rules. An edition with no configured offers directs players to **Earned Rewards** instead.

### Can I reduce the effects without losing progress?

Yes. `/ghosthunt effects reduced` lowers effects and removes close-encounter Darkness. `/ghosthunt effects off` hides the private effects entirely. Both modes retain discovery and reward eligibility.

## Good To Know

- The updated 2026 Ghost reward window includes November 10 and closes November 11 at 00:00 Europe/Amsterdam. Doors and Halloween Virus retain November 7. Check the in-game menu for installed availability.
- Hunting and Halloween travel end after November 1; extending rewards does not reopen the hunt.
- Completing all six Ghosts in a wave earns its kit even on a later hunt day. Finding all 24 earns all four wave kits and the bonus.
- Claim and shop in an allowed reward world with AutoSell off. Kit overflow drops nearby, so collect somewhere suitable.
- A delivered or uncertain purchase is not a new purchase opportunity. Follow the recovery message instead of buying again.
- Use `/ghosthunt`. The separate CMI `/ghost` emote does not forward plugin commands.
