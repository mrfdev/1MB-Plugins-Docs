This page introduces WorthAudit as a public staff guide. It focuses on what the tool does, how authorized staff get started, and which commands are useful.

## What It Does

Compare CMI worth with recipes, villager trades, observed sales, and ShopGUI+ prices.

## How Staff Use It

WorthAudit helps economy reviewers compare CMI worth values with recipes, villager trades, observed sales, and ShopGUI+ prices.

Available features include:

- CMI worth reviews for Paper recipes and supported vanilla villager trades.
- Observed CMI sales and comparisons with current worth values.
- ShopGUI+ listings and price differences with Markdown and YAML previews.

## Quick Start

1. Use `/worthaudit status` to see which audit modules are available.
2. Follow the [WorthAudit staff reference](/staff-reference/plugins/worthaudit/) for permissions, saved reports, and upgrade steps.
